**Faster**
- Writing a pipeline archive no longer holds back the game's pipeline builds. On a first start of Rise of the Tomb Raider, its compile threads waited 194 s in all for 27 writes and now don't wait at all: mean 21.5 instead of 20.1 fps while the archives fill, Geothermal Valley 10.5 instead of 7.5 fps.

**Fixed**
- Once a game had more than 32 pipeline archives, KosmicKrisp wrote all of the game's pipelines to new archives at every start, and the archives kept growing to 4–5 GB. Rise of the Tomb Raider's archives shrink from 5.0 GB to 860 MB at the first start with this version, and later starts take all pipelines from them and write none.

**Known issue**
- In tests, Rise of the Tomb Raider sometimes crashed or hung right when its benchmark would start: in 7 of 43 starts, but only with the pipeline archives and only when "BENCHMARK STARTEN" was clicked twice in quick succession. Clicked once, none of 15 starts failed. The cause is still open; `MESA_KK_PIPELINE_ARCHIVES=0` turns the archives off.

**For developers**
- Mesa patches 0041–0042; measurements and the crash details are in `docs/MACOS_PORT.md`.
- Wine's D3D9 tests and the D3D11 tests give the same results as with v0.1.0.16. vkd3d-proton's test suite runs about three times as fast with fresh archives and then fails two checks in `test_pipeline_library` and one in each of four `test_buffers_oob_behavior_*` tests: vkd3d-proton's disk-cache thread still holds a pipeline when the tests count references. With vkd3d-proton's disk cache off (`VKD3D_SHADER_CACHE_PATH=0`) they pass, and no other tests fail.

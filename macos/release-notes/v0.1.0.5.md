**New**
- Geometry shaders: Metal has no geometry shader stage, MoltenVK left them out so far, and what they emitted was missing, such as particles or triangles a shader discards or multiplies. MacProton now emulates them with compute shaders, for direct, indexed and indirect draws.

**Fixed**
- Medieval Dynasty with Direct3D 12 lost the graphics device a minute or two after loading the world. The cause were draws without a render target, which Unreal uses to compute shadows. The game now also runs with Direct3D 12; the launch option `-dx11` is no longer needed.
- Unreal Engine 4 games with Direct3D 11 showed a black world, only menus and UI were visible, such as Medieval Dynasty. The tonemapper's color grading is now generated correctly.
- Skin and hair in Horizon Zero Dawn were white. Shaders that sample shadows by comparison read their other textures only through the red channel.
- Horizon Zero Dawn crashed in the opening scene because two compute shaders were not translated for Metal.
- Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart aborted on start and then reported there was no graphics card. It now starts up to its launcher, and all pipelines are created. After that it still hangs.
- Tessellation under Direct3D 12: pipelines with packed shader inputs and outputs failed.
- Pixel shaders that write floating-point values to integer render targets made the whole pipeline fail.
- Out-of-bounds accesses to typed buffers now return 0 or are discarded, as under Direct3D, instead of hitting the memory next to them.

**For developers**
- `macos/smoke-test.py` starts all installed games one after another unattended and writes a report: window, image, crashes, device loss, failed pipelines and shaders Metal rejected.
- `macos/check-shaders.sh` compiles all shaders a game has used with the Metal compiler and lists which ones Metal rejects.
- `macos/tests/geometry/run.sh` checks the geometry shader emulation without a window.

**Still open**
- Geometry shaders together with tessellation, multiple streams and stream output.
- Games that require OpenGL 4.3 or later, such as Foundation, do not start, macOS offers at most OpenGL 4.1.

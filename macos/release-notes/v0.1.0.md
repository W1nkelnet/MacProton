First version of MacProton: Windows games from your Steam library on the Mac, with Wine, DXVK and MoltenVK.

**Installation:** Unzip, drag `MacProton.app` into Applications and allow it once, because the app is not notarized by Apple. Everything else is in the [README](https://github.com/W1nkelnet/MacProton#installation).

```bash
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MacProton.app
```

**New**
- Sync of the Steam library, download of the Windows versions with steamcmd
- Games with a Mac version run natively through Steam
- Direct3D 9 to 11 through DXVK and MoltenVK
- Steam sign-in in the app; the app downloads steamcmd itself on the first start
- Installs a game's runtime components, such as Visual C++, on its first start
- Steam reconnects automatically after downloads
- Saves through the Steam Cloud, controllers through SDL2
- Warnings about anti-cheat, third-party launchers and online requirements

**Known limitations**
- 32-bit Windows games crash on start
- No Direct3D 12, no Steam overlay
- Games with OpenGL 4.2 or later do not start, macOS offers at most OpenGL 4.1
- Online anti-cheat and third-party launchers (EA app, Ubisoft Connect) are not supported

Requires macOS 14 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon, Rosetta 2 and Steam for Mac.

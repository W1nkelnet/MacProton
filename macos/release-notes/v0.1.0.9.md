**New**
- KosmicKrisp presents frames through a Metal queue of its own. So far the GPU waited for the display before every frame and sat half idle. Medieval Dynasty runs in game at 22 instead of 14.5 fps, as fast as with MoltenVK. Green Hell runs at 23 fps, where MoltenVK manages 16.5.
- Games that render smaller than their window no longer make KosmicKrisp recreate the swapchain on every frame.
- KosmicKrisp stores compiled shaders on disk (`~/Library/Caches/MacProton`). From the second start on, compiling is skipped: on its second start Horizon Zero Dawn has 5,800 pipelines ready in under a minute. The first start now also compiles about 1.6 times as fast as before.
- KosmicKrisp reads empty texture slots from a real, empty texture instead of guarding every texture access separately. This alone raises Rise of the Tomb Raider (DirectX 12) in its built-in benchmark from 19 to 24 fps, and Metal compiles large shaders faster.
- Tessellation runs faster under KosmicKrisp, and buffers for tessellation and geometry shaders are reused instead of being created on every draw. In the Rise of the Tomb Raider benchmark (DirectX 12, High), the scene with the most tessellation reaches 32 instead of 29 fps; the average of all three scenes is about 27 fps.
- Rise of the Tomb Raider runs through its benchmark under DirectX 12; before, it crashed there (it still does on MoltenVK).
- The Rise of the Tomb Raider launcher no longer hangs now and then when closing.
- KosmicKrisp needs much less memory for large games: Horizon Zero Dawn took up to 18 GB while compiling its shaders and swapped on Macs with 16 GB; now it is a little over 4 GB.

**For developers**
- Mesa patches 0010–0012: a present queue of its own (`MESA_KK_PRESENT_QUEUE=0` turns it off), suboptimal only when the window size changed, `MESA_WSI_METAL_FPS=1` counts frames per second.
- Mesa patches 0013–0017: shader cache on disk, compressed MSL in memory, `MESA_KK_COMPILE_STATS=1` for compile times; 0017 (`MESA_KK_NULL_IMAGE_SELECT`) is replaced by 0020.
- Mesa patches 0018–0025: MSL without zero initialization, empty descriptors through zeroed textures (`MESA_KK_NULL_TEXTURES=0` switches back), R32G32B32 texel buffers, one disk cache per process, faster MSL output, more in `MESA_KK_COMPILE_STATS`.
- Mesa patches 0026–0028: `MESA_KK_CACHE_BUILD_ID` for developers, full SIMD groups in the tessellation emulation (and correct group counts for indirect tessellation draws), reused large command buffer allocations.
- `macos/fps.py` reads the frame counters of both drivers and the log of the Metal HUD; `macos/gpu-trace.py` counts frames from the present requests in a trace.
